## 2. Lindsay Adler Deep-Dive (Key Competitor)

Lindsay Adler (NYC fashion/beauty photographer, educator; lindsayadlerphotography.com) is the closest aspirational competitor: same niche (fashion/beauty/editorial), photographer-as-educator model.

**Her content distribution strategy (the thing to copy):**
1. **Own site = portfolio-as-SEO.** Each portfolio image page carries a keyword-rich caption + comma-separated keyword list (e.g., "beauty, photography, dramatic, lighting, fashion, portrait, makeup, vibrant, colors…") plus a 2–3 sentence descriptive paragraph. Every image is an indexable page.
2. **Education hub = learn.lindsayadlerphotography.com.** "Learn+" monthly episodes (e.g., "Headshots with Gary Hughes" — practical tips + real-world shooting challenges), plus paid guide bundles (Posing Guide Bundle, Ultimate Lighting Guide Bundle). Content marketing feeds product.
3. **Syndication on high-authority domains** — this is her real SEO moat:
   - **Adorama "Inside Fashion and Beauty Photography"** series: Tip 1/2/3 format, exact gear lists (Canon R5, RF 24-105mm), links to related guides, BTS video embeds. Clean, bold, graphic style carried through.
   - **Profoto collaborations** ("How to create a retro portrait", "Fine art portrait lighting"): **Step 1–4 lighting recipes with exact modifiers, positions, power settings AND camera settings** (Shutter 1/200, f/11, ISO 400). This exactness is why photographers bookmark, share, and link to her content.
   - **Adobe blog interview**: expert positioning ("collaborating with other artists brought up the level of my photography"), planning mantra: *"I do as much planning and pre-visualization as possible… then I let myself be creative in the moment."*
4. **Her book "The Photographer's Guide to Posing"** (bestseller since 2017) reveals her structural formula: **the 5 most common mistakes with before/after examples and specific fixes** → a **10-step framework** → per-subject chapters (women, men, couples, curvy, families, boudoir, maternity) → "Train Your Eye" exercises → full real-world session walkthroughs. *Teach the system, not just the moves.*

**What makes her content rank/share:**
- Exact technical recipes (modifier names, light positions, camera settings) — not vague "use soft light"
- Step-by-step numbered format with before/after imagery
- Problem → system → proof structure
- Cross-published on Adorama/Profoto/Adobe (domain authority she borrows)
- Every piece funnels to Learn+ (content → email/product)

**Takeaway for Jeff:** He can out-local her (Providence/RI/Boston angle she doesn't own) but should copy her *format*: exact settings, step-by-steps, before/after, mistake→fix structure.
