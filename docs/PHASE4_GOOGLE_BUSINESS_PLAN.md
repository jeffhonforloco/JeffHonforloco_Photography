# Phase 4 Google Business Profile plan

No third-party account was changed in this phase. The founder should execute this checklist in the profile owner account.

## Profile integrity

1. Use **Jeff Honforloco Photography** exactly.
2. Use the verified public phone and official HTTPS domain.
3. Choose the most accurate primary category available; add secondary categories only for services genuinely offered.
4. If clients are not received at a staffed, signed location during stated hours, configure a service-area business and hide the street address.
5. Keep Providence as the base; add service areas only where the business genuinely serves customers.
6. Complete hours, appointment requirements, accessibility and contact fields truthfully.

## Service and media cadence

- Add the nine published service families with concise descriptions consistent with the website.
- Upload a permissioned mix of finished work, behind-the-scenes images and a current logo; avoid duplicate or misleading stock imagery.
- Publish one useful update every two weeks: a real gallery, availability note, preparation tip or service explanation.
- Link each update to the most relevant canonical service page, not always the homepage.

## Reviews

- Send a neutral review request after final delivery to every eligible client.
- Never gate, purchase, prewrite or incentivize reviews.
- Respond to every review professionally without exposing private client details.
- Ask clients to describe the service and experience in their own words; never demand keywords.

## 30/60/90-day sequence

### First 30 days

- Correct identity fields and duplicates.
- Add services, appointment URL and a representative image set.
- Begin the post-delivery review request.
- Record baseline profile metrics and branded search screenshots.

### Days 31–60

- Publish two to four real updates.
- Answer recurring questions using facts already published on the site.
- Compare queries, actions and landing-page inquiries to baseline.
- Correct any directory still propagating old identity data.

### Days 61–90

- Expand the strongest service based on impressions and qualified leads.
- Publish a permissioned client story or local guide supported by firsthand work.
- Review media quality, review cadence and conversion by service.
- Keep, revise or stop tactics based on qualified inquiries—not vanity views.

## Measurement

Track calls, website clicks, booking-page sessions, direction requests (if applicable), messages, discovery queries, new reviews and qualified leads. Save a monthly export or screenshot because platform reporting windows can change.
